## Appendix: where each of the 288 requirement lines went

**Third draft, 14 Nov 2026.** This table agrees with the third draft of the definition, `process4_definition_v3.md`. Codes are allowed here. The line ids, their order, the short texts and the "disposition table" column are those of `process4_disposition.md`, unchanged from the second draft of this appendix.

**Who decided.** Every row that is ended, or whose way of being done is changed, names its decider. There are three kinds, and no row mixes them up:

- **The record of 13-14 Nov.** A decision listed in section B of the main text, cited here as "line n" of that section. These reached the definition through a session's written record, not through text Bob typed.
- **Bob's ledger.** An answer stored in his Keep and Drop Ledger.
- **Claude decided, 14 Nov.** A technical decision or a reading by Claude, reported to Bob, which he can overrule. No row gives one of these as Bob's.

A sentence a session wrote inside the record is not a decision of Bob's. The record's sentence "Kept Ledger lines this decision ends: ..." is the session's own. The main text treats it as Claude's consequence for the three checks beside a module's tests, and follows it for JOB-13 and TM-10. The rows do the same and say so.

**The "Now" column** holds one of seven statuses, with the decider where there is one. The "Flag" column holds the note: what changed, where a clause went, and anything the main text leaves unsaid.

**Place.** Section letters are the third draft's. For a reader holding the second draft: its B is now C, C is D, D is E, E is F, F is G, G is H, H is I, I is J, J is L and K is M. B (your decisions as recorded) and K (what does not exist yet) are new. "H" followed by a word is the theme in section H's list. "J n" is item n of section J and "L n" item n of section L. "M point n" is one of the eight earlier open points in section M. "M-tech n" is one of the six other technical points there. "H, by the count only" means the main text states the line nowhere: it stands only as one of the lines counted as kept. 24 rows are in that position.
