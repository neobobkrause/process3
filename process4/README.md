# Process4: definition work in progress

This folder holds the work of defining Process4, the successor to Process3. Nothing here is in force.

- `definition/`: the drafts of the definition (the highest number is current), the appendix of the 288 requirement lines, and the register of counter-arguments with their answers.
- `owner_words/`: the owner's messages of 14 Nov 2026, exactly as typed. Only these count as his word.
- `evidence/`: the post-mortem and audit files the definition rests on, as handed over on 14 Nov 2026.
- `working/`: workers' notes and the offline replay of Process3's recorded interface changes (scripts and results). Working notes use reference codes and are not written for the owner.
