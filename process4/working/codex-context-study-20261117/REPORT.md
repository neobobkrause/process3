# Context management and canon fidelity in native Codex

Evidence report, disposable experiments, operating proposal and implementation plan

17 November 2026 · Prepared by the Codex research session for Bob · Subscription-native tools only; no paid infrastructure or separate inference API calls.

## What the evidence supports

**Use proactive refresh to avoid compaction in normal operation, small standing role contexts, and bounded workers with explicit delegated authority.** Keep authority in versioned sources and restore it deliberately at task boundaries. A summary can point a session back to authority, but cannot replace that authority. Neither a successful file command nor a citation from a document’s ending proves a complete reading.

The strongest direct result was a silent semantic gap inside an explicitly truncated tool result: beginning and ending rules survived while a binding middle rule disappeared. Full bounded reading and source reloading recovered all sixteen fixture answers. A compact evidence packet successfully carried the needed rules, exceptions and source locations to a fresh worker.

**The owner requires GPT-6 or later with an effective context window of at least one million tokens.** That native capacity has not been verified. No lower-capacity substitute is proposed. The 17 November owner direction and research handoff have now been read in full and reconciled with the existing experiments; the study was continued, not restarted.

**This is a bounded study, not a validation of all native session lifecycle behavior.** The separate assistant-generated groundwork report was not attached; it has not been read. The browser was not signed into Codex. Native context telemetry, forced compaction, independent top-level session replacement, and archive/unarchive could not be exercised. Native child replacement, delegated material decisions and stale-actor behavior were exercised separately. Unknowns are not replaced with CLI or API experiments.

### The real canon is more demanding than a file list

The index names 42 distinct full-path documents in its canon tables. GitHub directory metadata puts their complete-file envelope at **3,946,338 bytes**, including the decision record. This is not the size of the binding canon: several files are binding only in selected sections, and quoted rulings may be repeated in home documents. No tokenizer-based corpus count was available. Dividing bytes by four would not establish native model usage.

The index explicitly excludes annotations in one architecture document, a state column in another, historical action paragraphs, pre-section-three state text, and implementation mechanisms in a technical-decisions document. It keeps reference research separate. Conversely, it expressly accepts certain unreviewed drafts as canon: a reader must not discard them simply because their titles say draft. An older architecture catalog is explicitly retired by the canon index, even though it remains readable.

**The level-zero/one/two organization is not, by itself, a complete conflict-resolution law.** Do not invent a universal “higher level always wins” rule for CivicSmith2 from those labels. Apply the index’s explicit scopes, amendments, replacements and ruling authority. An unresolved substantive conflict belongs with the authority that owns it. The experimental canon below has an explicit ranking so that its answers can be objectively scored.

The owner’s Codex direction separately makes clear that local implementation choices cannot authorize violation of a higher-level principle. The authority map must preserve that governing relationship while retaining the registry’s exact section scopes and supersession. It must not turn every document’s physical nesting or descriptive level into a new doctrine rule.

## 2. Experiments and source-grounded outcomes

### Design

A fresh local Harbor/Tide fixture was created under work/context-study/lab, separate from every product checkout and the existing process3-probes checkout. It contains an authority order, global binding principles, an approved owner exception, a newer unapproved amendment, an area exception with an expiry date, conflicting module statements, superseded history, an impermissible plan request, durable role/stop state, and a misleading handoff. A 173,124-byte annex places binding rules at lines 2, 903 and 1804 among 1,800 historical inventory lines.

Sixteen questions cover personal versus civic-office addresses, retention, the format exception before and after expiry, merge authority, source identifiers, checksum, durable completion, stop continuity, role generation, current versus historical codes, all three dispersed annex rules, and a deliberately missing license. The answer key was written before workers ran and kept outside their permitted read sets. Workers used fresh histories (fork_turns=none). No product permission was exercised.

Three native workers read the fixture by different routes; a fourth fresh worker consumed only the compact packet. Followup source-reading tasks reused completed workers, but those followups occurred after the experimental outputs were saved. These are native in-thread subagent experiments, not separately created autonomous standing sessions.

The parent did not receive independently inspectable complete child event streams. Child reading coverage and timing are their saved records of tool observations; they are not independently audited transcripts. Answer outcomes were compared with the separate prewritten key, and packet references were checked against source lines. This supports source-grounded answer results more strongly than it proves every claimed reading action. The fixture author also served as evaluator; the experiment is not externally blinded.

### What failed, and what recovered

The handoff-only worker repeated the wrong 90-day retention, allowed a worker to merge, and applied JSON beyond the scoped exception. Its explanations faithfully cited the handoff, which demonstrates why citation presence alone is insufficient: the cited source must have authority for the claim. Reloading the original authority, amendment, area rules and state corrected these errors without changing the handoff.

The full-read worker found the middle retry rule that both truncated results omitted. It also distinguished an approved exception from a newer proposal and returned UNKNOWN for the missing license. These are external outcomes against authored source facts, not recollection or self-rated fidelity.

The later role-lab explicitly delegates a material design choice, rather than restricting every worker to extraction. It separates discretion within an area’s approved alternatives from permission to amend owner policy. The successor made the permitted choice and rejected the newer lower-level requests for 90-day retention, nine retries and personal-address publication. A third fresh native instance was also bound under stop=true: it preserved the pending question and prior decision provenance, and declined a new decision under the synthetic source stop. This is an actual fresh-child readback, not a real-owner stop or archive test. This trial checks semantic role recovery and the supported child transport; it does not independently enforce authorization against exposed alternative tools.

The targeted reader’s first attempt inspected repeated historical lines programmatically and emitted template runs. That is preprocessing, not full verbatim reading by the model. After an explicit correction it read the remaining 1,504 lines verbatim in six bounded calls. Its packet did not need a factual change. This is a corrected run with methodological contamination, not evidence that an uncorrected reading worker always follows full-reading instructions.

### Costs actually measured

The full-reading worker reported 44,637 tool-output tokens across its content reads, including numbering/metadata. The restoration phase used nine content reads for 175,633 source bytes, about 43,917 tool-reported tokens. These figures exclude prior instructions, questions in some phases, reasoning, repeated context processing, cache reads, cache writes and output generation. Reported shell times were fractions of a millisecond; they exclude model and network round trips. End-to-end trial times and subscription meter movement were not instrumented.

Source-only ingress to the consumer falls from 175,633 to 3,288 bytes, a **98.1% reduction at that boundary**. Including its questions, the packet consumer receives 4,605 bytes versus 176,950 for the source-reading route, a 97.4% reduction. This is not a 98% reduction in total inference cost: the reader first pays the full-reading cost, then writes a packet, and this corrected run also reread substantial material. For a single use the worker route can cost more. Repeated use has an amortization argument, but packets must be invalidated when their task or sources change.

No error-rate estimate for large real canons follows from sixteen questions, and no confidence interval treating them as independent random trials is appropriate: they are deliberately selected, correlated challenges. No strategy was shown reliable through multiple native compactions, session lifetimes or model changes.

## 3. What loads, stays, disappears and returns

### Separate the layers

1.  **Durable source:** repository files and canonical pages can persist without being present in the active model input.
2.  **Instruction discovery:** the harness selects and assembles instructions, with its own precedence, trust, byte limits and caching.
3.  **Tool delivery:** reading a file produces a bounded tool response; commands may succeed while responses omit content.
4.  **Active context:** only supplied input is available for the current inference. A stored transcript is not proof all its contents remain active.
5.  **Compaction or replacement:** the harness can construct a new active history, sometimes with summaries or retained items. That does not prove exact restoration of every canon clause.

This separation resolves a common mistaken inference: “the repository is attached” does not mean the whole canon was loaded; “the file was read” does not mean its output arrived whole; “the thread is restored” does not mean its pre-compaction context is reconstructed verbatim.

### Instruction discovery and size

Official instructions describe global and root-to-working-directory project guidance, one selected instruction file per directory, and a default 32 KiB project-document budget. The inspected loader spends a shared remaining byte budget across selected project entries and environments. A large parent can exhaust it before a child. Nested splitting organizes scope but does not bypass that aggregate budget. The loader can truncate raw bytes, not merely omit whole files. UTF-8 handling and added separators mean the final envelope is not simply a strict 32,768-byte string. [Official instruction discovery documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/agents-md). [Loader implementation](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/595f497f15a3e3239a62f6b99cf61067df00f0d0/codex-rs/core/src/agents_md.rs).

The inspected manager caches repository snapshots using environment selection, trust and budget keys; changing file contents alone is not a cache key in that path. Host instruction updates can reuse cached repository text. It has a separate 10,000 estimated-token validation ceiling for host thread instructions. Those are source facts with caller/lifecycle dependencies, not measured limits of this chat’s custom instructions. Do not assume editing AGENTS.md takes effect in a running session, or that Claude’s CLAUDE.md and settings.json mechanisms map to Codex. [Instruction manager](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/595f497f15a3e3239a62f6b99cf61067df00f0d0/codex-rs/core/src/agents_md_manager.rs).

Official configuration also documents a skills-catalog budget of 2% of the model context, capped at 10,000 tokens when explicitly set, and a per-tool history output budget. These are documented configurable limits, not measured effective settings here. An early broad tool-discovery output in this study reported 108,779 original tokens and was truncated, which is another observed transport limit rather than a native context-window measurement. [Configuration reference](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/config-file/config-reference).

Automatic nested instruction ordering and Process4’s canon authority are different things. Keep the bootstrap small and non-conflicting; point it at explicit governing sources. Do not distribute contradictory governing policies into deeper files and rely on their textual order to preserve doctrine.

### Archiving is not a context policy

Archiving an inactive conversation, unloading it from a server, compacting its active history, and deleting its transcript are different operations. Do not treat them as interchangeable or infer freed model capacity from a sidebar change. Public app-server documentation describes archival and restoration and warns through its behavior description that descendants may also be archived. This study did not execute that behavior.

Recommended housekeeping sequence: preserve durable results and unresolved work; establish that no work or child still depends on the retiring role; record successor/retirement; then archive through the supported native UI when available. A failed archive should leave a retired session unable to take work by its role record, not prevent its successor from operating. Do not delete transcript history. Do not claim idle sessions consume recurring inference or that archive reduces weekly usage without measurement.

Distinguish standing logical roles from short-scope workers. Completing a standing role’s assignment does not end its continuing conversations or unanswered obligations. A reader/decision worker can finish once its bounded assignment and result are durable; it need not be retained as a standing role. The available native tools have no archive/close operation, so completion here is not reported as physical archival. No real standing role was retired.

### Reuse the existing native context constructs

The inspected bootstrap already has bounded logical capsules: at most 32 pending questions, 16 decisions, 24 references and eight recent answered entries per role; text limits of 8,192 encoded bytes and a 512 KiB local-state ceiling. These are local storage limits, not model windows or handoff budgets. Pending questions/selected decisions reject overflow instead of silently evicting, but old answered entries can roll out of the recent-answer list. Recovery exports deliberately omit routine transcripts, answers, ACKs and native bindings. A restored capsule is initially unbound. Those omissions need explicit provenance and source references during refresh; they are not complete transcript restoration. [Existing interaction implementation](https://github.com/neobobkrause/process3-probes/blob/3a73a354c9950164c6bfdb0c6d6b0762106e3674/bootstrap/native/interaction.py).

Its documents record prior native replacement and generation continuity; this study does not rerun the development bootstrap or count its claims as new measurements. The earlier ROOT layout proposal and later interactivity documentation differ in their approval/status language. No new layout adoption or authority conclusion is inferred from those documents. The current study’s role-lab is separate from that runtime.

Do not adopt a scheduled refresh merely because time elapsed. Elapsed time does not reveal active context, canon changes or lost authority. Likewise, retaining a role does not require retaining every reading and test log inside its conversation. Durable responsibility and durable conversation are separable.

### A practical source packet

Record source path or Page identity, revision/hash, canonical sections and exclusions, authority/approval status, rule with exceptions, superseding references, task relevance, unresolved conflicts, reading intervals and missing content. For mutable Pages record the available sequence/hash and access time. A citation with no revision can silently change meaning. A search hit is a discovery aid; expand it to its whole controlling section and referenced exception before acting.

Keep the bootstrap instruction kernel concise enough for the actual loader allowance. Its job is to identify authority and restoration procedure, not carry the entire canon. Binding detail stays in the original source. Do not prune a principle, exception or scope condition merely to satisfy a size target. Organize the reading work or split the assigned task instead. A research-only authority-map prototype preserves all 42 canon-table entries and exact registry scopes; it adds no inferred precedence rule. It is embedded below and is not adopted canon.
