## Capsule limits and retention

- `bootstrap/native/interaction.py:15`–`22`: maximum 128 logical roles; per-role maximum 32 pending questions, 16 decisions, 24 references, and 8 recently answered entries; default nonempty text limit 8,192 encoded bytes; local state ceiling 512 KiB. Pending answers reserve `2 * 8192 + 2048 = 18,432` bytes per pending question. These are local data-structure limits, not a native model context-window size or active-worker concurrency allowance.
- `bootstrap/native/interaction.py:32`–`35` validates nonempty text by encoded byte length. Most identifiers have explicit 256-byte limits (`:71`–`72`, `:146`); generations are positive integers below 2^63 (`:38`–`40`). `_change` deep-copies and rejects aggregate capacity before replacing state (`:43`–`53`), so failed mutations preserve pending context. JSON sizing here includes default serializer formatting and pending reserves (`:49`–`50`); file persistence separately checks compact serialized size (`:309`–`310`).
- Capsule memory is deliberately selected knowledge, not automatic transcript retention (`bootstrap/native/interaction.py:129`–`140`). Duplicate decisions/references are ignored; full knowledge lists reject additions rather than silently evicting. Pending-question overflow rejects without discarding (`:160`–`162`). Processing ACKs leave questions pending (`:166`–`175`) and permit at most eight receipts per question (`:174`). A substantive correlated answer removes its pending entry and appends answer evidence, but only the latest eight answers remain (`:179`–`192`). Thus “no silent pending loss” does not mean all answered history is retained forever.
- Bind replacement retains logical context/questions (`bootstrap/native/interaction.py:65`–`99`): conversation identity cannot change (`:78`–`79`), generation must increase for a bound replacement (`:85`–`86`), and an explicitly retired role cannot silently return (`:77`). Generation-sensitive actions reject stale sessions (`:56`–`61`). There is no time-based retention expiry in these files; retention is bounded by capacity, explicit retirement, and recovery filtering.
- Recovery snapshots retain identity, disposition, assignment, decisions, references, pending questions, and explicit retirement context (`bootstrap/native/interaction.py:223`–`236`). They intentionally omit routine transcripts, answers, ACKs, and native bindings. Restore validates schema/caps and cannot overwrite a live local role (`:239`–`290`); imported roles are unbound, counters reset, answered history empty, receipts empty (`:285`–`288`). Stored identity is not evidence of a live native binding (`:240`–`243`). This is a selective handoff, not complete history replay.
- Local persistence uses a lock, temporary file, flush/fsync, replace, and directory fsync (`bootstrap/native/interaction.py:300`–`321`). Documentation explicitly disclaims routine remote persistence and physical/authentication isolation (`:293`–`297`); host loss needs a meaningful recovery snapshot. Source is evidence of intended implementation, not execution of durability guarantees here.

## Authority and worker boundaries

- Technical instructions explicitly are not owner grants or adoption decisions (`bootstrap/role_instructions.py:1`, `:60`). Common rules preserve exact owner instructions and unfinished work (`:7`), treat owner grants as authority (`:8`), prohibit expanding grants, paid/metered inference and unauthorized main/real-tranche operations (`:9`–`11`), and use ephemeral messaging/local operational mailboxes rather than GitHub heartbeat/message writes (`:12`). These are instructions in this experiment, not additional authority for the present study.
- ROOT supervises liveness, not product decisions (`bootstrap/role_instructions.py:21`–`23`); coordinator relays exact marked owner instructions and does not turn questions into builds (`:30`–`32`); ARCH owns module decisions, ordering, file ownership and restricted integration (`:35`–`39`). JOB writes only its assigned module/tests and may not create sessions, merge, deploy or alter process/configuration (`:42`–`44`). Design LANE may handle assigned design paths but not product implementation or grant expansion (`:47`–`48`). PROCESS proposals require owner authority for adoption (`:51`–`52`).
- No numeric active-worker/concurrency cap is implemented by the three read files. The proposal says actual native concurrency can be lower than the owner's account job-slot cap and dispatch must narrow without changing that cap (`bootstrap/native/ROOT-LAYOUT-PROPOSAL.md:61`–`64`). The 128-role capsule limit must not be equated with 128 simultaneous workers.

## Native lifecycle assumptions and recorded observations

- Source documentation assumes completed native turns remain reachable by followup (`bootstrap/native/interaction.py:1`–`5`). Role instructions distinguish turn completion, assignment completion, idle, replacement, and explicit retirement (`bootstrap/role_instructions.py:3`), use followup to reactivate idle/completed sessions, and say `send_message` alone does not wake idle recipients (`:4`). This is an experimental lifecycle contract/assumption, not a universal platform guarantee established by this reading.
- Explicit events require evidence and distinguish assignment/turn state (`bootstrap/native/interaction.py:102`–`126`). Retirement requires reason, source, and evidence, while outstanding questions remain inspectable (`:195`–`203`). Those string fields describe trusted-host observations; the module explicitly says they do not authenticate an owner or prove delivery (`:3`–`5`).
- `delivery_intent` is only a plan: unbound/retired/unknown-availability roles hold, known idle/completed/running/retained-unlisted roles produce a followup target plus generation and stable identity, with `requires_actual_submission=True` (`bootstrap/native/interaction.py:210`–`220`). Inventory absence alone never retires or replaces a known session. `bind` likewise requires an actual native event, not missing inventory (`:65`–`69`).
- The proposal records, without independently proving here, that the tested depth0 chat had not demonstrated message-triggered idle wake; a child result prepared about 72 seconds before scheduled entry was presented afterward, tested child inventory lacked notify_parent, and CLI queue lacked a reachable binding/default daemon (`bootstrap/native/ROOT-LAYOUT-PROPOSAL.md:3`–`8`). It explicitly avoids universal impossibility claims.
- Proposed layout is console depth0, distinct retained ROOT depth1, ARCH/coordinator depth2, JOB depth3, watcher sibling depth1 (`bootstrap/native/ROOT-LAYOUT-PROPOSAL.md:12`–`28`). The document reports a segregated read-only fixture creating the full chain, waking idle supervisory depth1 in 11.139 seconds, and a synthetic coordinator question without build/session creation (`:30`–`34`). It reports active-console replacement of generation1 with sibling generation2, retained-ARCH contact across old lineage, and JOB wake across changed path in 13.542 seconds (`:36`–`41`). These are document-recorded model/tool timings to first shell action, expressly not SLA/service latency; the referenced aggregate evidence (`:43`–`44`) was not read.
- Limits remain: depth0 reactive wake is not solved; idle owner-visible notification may wait; replacement was shown only with console active and otherwise needs a safety wake; no instant failover/deleted-chat resurrection is claimed (`bootstrap/native/ROOT-LAYOUT-PROPOSAL.md:50`–`60`). Alternative tools remain, so no physical credential/tool isolation is established (`:65`–`66`).
- Approval/version boundary: the proposal is titled awaiting owner decision (`bootstrap/native/ROOT-LAYOUT-PROPOSAL.md:1`), says production role registrations have not been remapped (`:67`–`70`), and asks approval for the ROOT identity interpretation (`:79`–`84`). Role instructions discuss the layout conditionally as “owner-approved” or “in addressable ROOT mode” (`bootstrap/role_instructions.py:16`–`18`, `:21`, `:26`). These texts alone do not demonstrate that approval occurred or that the proposed mode is deployed. Preserving functional role authority does not make a replacement child identical to the existing depth0 session.
