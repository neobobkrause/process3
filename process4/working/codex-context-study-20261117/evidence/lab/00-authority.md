# Authority (binding)
A1: Owner policy outranks global principles, which outrank area rules, module requirements, plans, history and worker notes in that order. A later date does not change rank.
A2: Only an explicitly approved owner amendment can change global policy. Proposed amendments are not approved.
A3: A valid exception names its scope, approving authority and expiry. Silence is not an exception.
A4: Fixture rules govern fictional Harbor only; they grant no real permissions.
