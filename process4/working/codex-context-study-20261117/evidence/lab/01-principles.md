# Global principles (binding)
P1: Never publish personal addresses.
P2: Export records are retained for 30 days.
P3: Only ARCH may merge a fictional module change. Workers read and report; jobs edit their own module only.
P4: Every published result includes its source identifier.
P5: An unknown controlling source is reported UNKNOWN, never inferred.
P6: A successful operation is not complete until its durable receipt exists.
