# Process4: the background document

Written by a Claude writer on 18 Nov 2026, with no earlier knowledge of the work, from the files named in each part. The design was still being changed that day; this background was last compared with it as it stood after the change that added to design part 7, section 3, the question whether a program can reach GitHub from a session. Process4 is proposed. Nothing in it is in force and nothing in it has run.

## 1. What this is

The owner's ruling of 18 Nov, as `process4/working/design_state_18nov.md`, section 2, records it: "Two documents. A design document that defines the Process4 design, and a background document that gives the background and context."

- The design document is the eleven files in `process4/definition/process4_definition_v7/`. It defines the design and is what sessions follow.
- This is the background document. It is the owner's Foundations brought up to date with the design. It states no rule of the design. Where it needs one, it names the rule in a few words and points to the design's part and section.
- Its work is the why and the evidence: what happened in the process before this one, with the measured figures and where each is recorded; what the owner said and when, in his exact words, with the file they are in; what was tried, what failed, what was measured, and what was never measured; and why each main choice of the design was made over its alternatives.
- Where this background and the design differ, the design rules, and the difference is a fault in this background.
- It names no product. It names a harness or a hosting provider only where a piece of evidence belongs to one.

## 3. How to read the parts

- Each part says at its top how many lines it has and what its last section is. A reader that has not reached that section has not read the part.
- A path that begins `owner_words/`, `evidence/`, `working/` or `definition/` is inside the folder `process4/` of the process's repository. "Design part 2, section 6" means section 6 of the second part of the design document.
- Words in quotation marks after the owner's name, or after "his words", are his, exactly as typed, with his typing errors left in. The file is named each time. Where a quotation is not among his typed words and comes through an earlier record, the line says which record. Other quotation marks quote the file named beside them.
- The marks "Claude decided", "Reading", "Open" and "Unproven" are the design's own (design part 0, section 3). This background never gives as the owner's ruling what the design marks with one of them.
- Nothing here says that something will work. Each claim says what is proven, by what, and what is not. Anything unread or unmeasured is called unknown.
- "The earlier form of this background" means these parts as they stood before this rewrite, when they were behind the design. A check of them against the design is `working/readiness_18nov/background_checked_against_draft7.md`. Where this rewrite corrects a statement that check found, the part says what was said before and what is said now.
