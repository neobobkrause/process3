## How each kind arose, as far as the files show

- **Quotations not his (A).** Each came into the design through a file a session wrote: the session's record of 13 and 14 Nov, the Foundations, Process3's decision record, the design state of 18 Nov, and the handoff of 18 and 19 Nov. Later drafts repeated the words without the file that held them, and part 0:21 then declared every quotation his. No file of his own words exists for 18 or 19 Nov, so what he said on those days survives only inside Claude's summaries. The two sentences called "his written instructions" are the Foundations' own, addressed to him.
- **Rules presented as his (B).** The design state of 18 Nov was "Written by Claude from Bob's answers", under headings such as "Testing (Bob's ruling, closed)", "Working principles (Bob, 18 Nov)" and "Decided from ideas (Bob, 18 Nov)"; the last section's ideas were a Claude worker's proposals (`working/owner_words_ideas_18nov.md`). The seventh draft took its lines in with no mark, and part 0:27 makes an unmarked line his ruling. The same rule covers the 34 rules carried unmarked from the sixth draft, which part 0:27 admits he never ruled on one by one.
- **Approvals sentences (C).** On 18 or 19 Nov he rejected a freshly written wording ("The text you're suggesting here Doesn't look to be sufficient"), and the handoff turned that into the instruction "Do not propose permission wording that is not an edit of his own Process3 wording" (`working/handoff_18nov_walkthrough_session.md:30`). So each block was written as an edit of his first-person text, and what Process4 needed was written into it in the same first person: "They merge nothing. I merge each of their pull requests myself", "the one session I start". Each change is listed in a table beside the block, but the block itself carries no mark. Once he committed the block exactly as written, it read as his own text, and a later session quoted it back to him as "your own approvals". The "I merge" sentence itself comes from part 4:156 of the design, marked "Claude decided".
