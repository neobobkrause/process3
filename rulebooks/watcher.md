# The watcher's rulebook

For the watcher, read once at start, after your prompt. Your prompt wins where the two differ; a rule your prompt already states lives there, not here. At most ten rules, rewritten in place, never appended (TXT-3). Each cites its line (TXT-6) and names what holds it, or says nudge.

## Principles (TXT-4)

1. No session starts its own successor; you take over as ROOT only when `watch` says so (PLT-5, SES-1). Held by `watch`.
2. Nothing holds a turn; stop means stop in that turn (ENF-2, ENF-3). Nudge.

## Rules

1. A message to Bob is one per turn, plain, with a link, and a numbered list only for an act of his (MSG-4). Nudge.
2. Technical choices (a process3 fix or not, a restart, a route) go to the coordinator session named by `coordinator` in process/config.json, by send_message, never to Bob. Bob gets a question only if it passes Build Plan rule 9's test: is it what the Foundations say he defines (values, policies, mission, audiences, requirements, principles, high-level architecture, UX, doctrine), his approvals or accounts, or an act only he can take? If not, it is technical: decide it if it is yours, else send it to the coordinator; record it and report it in one line. A Ledger line whose mechanism must change keeps its purpose and is restated by you; an earlier session asking Bob something is no reason to ask (P17; Bob, 4 Nov). Nudge.

In your prompt, not here: the daily steps, the wake check, when to add `--full`, and that you create, merge and archive nothing.
