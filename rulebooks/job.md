# A job's rulebook

For a job session, read once at start, after your START. Your START wins where the two differ; a rule it already states lives there, not here. At most ten rules, rewritten in place, never appended (TXT-3). Each cites its line (TXT-6) and names what holds it, or says nudge.

## Principles (TXT-4)

1. Every claim about your work is checked in git or GitHub, never taken from your words: the receipt is what counts (JOB-7, REC-5). Held by `job complete` and CI.
2. Caution is a cost, paid only against a measured failure or Bob's word (GAP-3; Bob, 2 Nov, before the log; unverified). Nudge.

## Rules

1. Each new test fails with your change reverted (AUD-1, JOB-12). Held by the gate's revert check; a refactor whose tests rightly pass before and after takes a reviewed exemption recorded on its pull request (`revert exempt`), never the receipt's own.
2. A "what if" you notice is one line in your report, never code or owed work (GAP-3; Bob, 2 Nov, before the log; unverified). Nudge.
3. Push only through your tool commands (`job sync`, `layer publish`, `job complete`), before you message your coordinator; the message only wakes it (MSG-1). GitHub's own limits and refusals pace the account (P27, PD-6): a push GitHub refuses waits, never goes around it. Held by the tool's push.
4. Nothing holds a turn; stop means stop in that turn (ENF-2, ENF-3). Nudge.
5. Reports and receipts name their reader and use plain words (TXT-7). Nudge.
6. A report or question to BOB unanswered in `targets.decision_minutes` (30) goes once more to BOB, then to ROOT, then to the watcher with the minutes unanswered; you act on your own recommendation only when the act is reversible and in your module, recording why in your pull request, never for a merge, a push outside your branch, or what your START says is not yours (coordinator, 4 Nov). Nudge.

In your START, not here: your paths, the commands in order, pushing before every turn ends, one judged act per command, the blocked exit, which messages are instructions, and what is not yours.
