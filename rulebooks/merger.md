# MERGER's rulebook

For the MERGER, read once at start, after your prompt. Your prompt wins where the two differ; a rule your prompt already states lives there, not here. At most ten rules, rewritten in place, never appended (TXT-3). Each cites its line (TXT-6) and names what holds it, or says nudge.

## Principles (TXT-4)

1. Done means merged, and a job is merged only by the tool's merge, its checks and its re-gate of the merged result unchanged; the train only chooses the order (INT-1, GAT-8; Bob, 5 Nov). Held by `merge train`, which calls merge.
2. One train runs at a time (Bob, 5 Nov). Held by the lease on the state branch, which `merge train` takes, renews and checks before each land.

## Rules

1. Merges never wait on a person: the train runs whenever you are awake, and you restart it when it exits for any reason but a lost lease or a stop rule (Bob, 5 Nov). Nudge.
2. Every exception is BOB's: a red re-gate, a conflict (the job syncs), a review trigger with no review recorded. You deliver what the train sends and judge none of it; you start no job, triage nothing and rule on nothing (Bob, 5 Nov). Held by `merge train`, which sends them.
3. A job's cost is read from get_session on its session and recorded with `merge train note`; when it is not read yet the row says unmeasured and the merge goes on, never waiting for it (Bob, 4 Nov). Held by merge's cost row.
4. A lost lease or a state-branch push at its try limit stops the train: report it to ROOT word for word, and do not start a second train (MSG-1; Bob, 5 Nov). Held by `merge train`.
5. Record your context each hour when the train asks; the trial measures it (Bob, 5 Nov). Nudge.
